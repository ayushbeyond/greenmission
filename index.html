Questions

Before you
book.

When do slots open, and when does the convoy leave?

Rider onboarding opens across India on 19.11. The flag-off date is revealed only once the convoy is complete and every rider has been trained. Until then, NMT promotion rides go out across Indore every day.

Who can join?

Anybody with a road-legal motorcycle or car, a valid licence and insurance. Families, colleges, schools, corporates, hospitality teams, growers and creators can join the movement too.

How are convoy spots allotted?

In booking order. The earlier you book, the further up the convoy you ride. Cars ride in the left lane, bikes in the right.

What does the ₹50,000 onboarding include?

Your rider badge, the premium rider kit (worth around ₹30,000), the NMT Home Kit, camp nights, priority access across the route, your convoy spot, daily promotion rides in Indore before departure, a participation certificate and access to the Green Mission India network.

Is the ride sponsored?

No, strictly no sponsors. NMT Green Ride is funded by the people who join it. The aim is to help make Indore one of India's greenest cities.

Where do we stay?

The ride camps. There are no hotel stays in the package; nights are camp nights with the convoy. If you prefer a hotel on some nights, you can book it yourself. Dates and stops may shift, so keep plans flexible.

Can I join from my own city?

Yes. Riders can join in Indore, Neemuch, Mumbai, Hyderabad or Coimbatore, or ride in from anywhere else and meet the convoy on the route. Tell us your city when you book.

Is it safe?

Every 10 riders have a Green Captain and a deputy, all experienced riders. The convoy travels with a doctors' team, chefs, security, GPS tracking and walkie-talkies, and the ride is streamed live on the site, Instagram and YouTube.

Can I support without riding?

Yes. Contribute any amount, there is no minimum and no maximum. Message us on WhatsApp and we send the UPI scanner.

What do I wear?

White top and blue jeans. Protective riding gear is mandatory for every rider.
